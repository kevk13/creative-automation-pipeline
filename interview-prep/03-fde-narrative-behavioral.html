FDE: Narrative + Behavioral

The code is the easy part of a final round. This decides it. Fill the YOUR STORY slots before the call.

30-second opening

"Marketing teams need hundreds of on-brand variants per campaign, and by hand it's too slow and expensive.

I built a 6-step pipeline: a brief in YAML or JSON goes in, compliance-checked creatives come out.

Load the brief, gather or generate each image, render 3 aspect ratios, apply the brand overlay, organize outputs, check compliance.

Every step has a typed contract, every Claude call logs cost. It's a deliberate 2-3 hour POC, so I scoped out auth, queuing, and multi-tenancy on purpose.

Happy to go deep wherever you want."

Then stop talking. Let them steer.

Behavioral, framed for FDE

Why a customer-facing FDE role? Why Adobe? do you actually want this, or is it a fallback. YOUR REASON + "I like owning the line from a messy customer requirement to a shipped answer. Adobe is where creative tooling and GenAI meet at enterprise scale."
Ambiguous or shifting requirements. do you scope, or boil the ocean. YOUR STORY + "The brief format wasn't given, so I defined the Zod contract myself and made it the source of truth. I picked the 80% path and documented the rest as deferred."
A technical tradeoff under time pressure. can you decide and defend, not just list options. pick one: polling over SSE (proxy buffered chunks) / deterministic compliance as the trusted gate, vision as advisory / failure isolation in compliance but not generation, a conscious scope cut.
Customer wants something the product can't do. do you push back well and offer a path. YOUR STORY + "I separate the need from the asked-for feature, confirm the need, then offer the closest supported path plus the cost of the bespoke one."
Disagreeing with a stakeholder. can you hold a position without being difficult. YOUR STORY + "I make it concrete and testable, then defer to data. If someone said 'just trust the vision check,' I'd show it has no seed and flips between runs."

Talk-track reminders

  • Lead with the customer problem, not the tech.
  • Name your scope cuts before they do. "I deferred X on purpose" = senior.
  • Tradeoffs explicit: "I chose A over B because C, and here's when I'd revisit."
  • Show observability: typed contracts, structured logs, cost ledger.
  • Don't know? Say so, then say how you'd find out.

Ask them (pick 2–3)

  • "How much of an FDE engagement is greenfield building vs. integrating into the customer's existing Adobe stack?"
  • "Where does the FDE handoff to core engineering happen, and what makes it go badly?"
  • "What separates an FDE thriving here at one year from one who's struggling?"
  • "How much of the role is GenAI solutioning vs. classic integration today, and where's that heading?"
  • "What customer problem is your team fighting right now that you wish was solved?"
