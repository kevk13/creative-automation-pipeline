One-liner positioning

"I built this neutral on purpose so the pattern is portable. The architecture, a typed brief contract, then gather, render, brand, and compliance as isolated steps, drops straight onto Firefly Services for generation and GenStudio for orchestration. The seams I designed, the storage adapter and the single generateImage() function, are exactly where Adobe's APIs would plug in."

Scale-to-prod on Adobe (if they push)

  • Generation: Firefly Services, with custom-trained brand models per client where available.
  • Orchestration + durability: a real job queue and a runs table, fronting GenStudio-style variant generation.
  • Brand + legal: managed brand checks + Content Credentials for provenance, replacing my three heuristics.
  • Review: Frame.io approval gates before assets are published.
  • Asset lifecycle: AEM Assets as the system of record for reuse and governance.
